## Sequence Alignment

- Software: MEGA12
- Alignment method: MUSCLE

## Phylogenetic Analysis

- Tree construction method: Neighbor-Joining (NJ)

## Observation

The reconstructed tree represents the evolutionary relationships among the four selected COL1A1 sequences.
